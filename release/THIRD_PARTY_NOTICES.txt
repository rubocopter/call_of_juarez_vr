Adapted reload cartridge artwork (embedded in the VR DLL)
The active loose .357 cartridge mesh and albedo are adapted from the 357Bullet
node in "Revolver Game Asset" by loafbrr_1 (December 4, 2021), under CC0 1.0.
The package embeds the selected geometry, normals/UVs and a cropped albedo bake;
the runtime adapts the original orange case palette to brass with preview shading.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/revolver-game-asset
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The earlier loose .44 Magnum geometry from Pichuliru's "CC0 Flat Ammunition"
(April 4, 2022), also CC0 1.0, is retained for the legacy cartridge visual.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-flat-ammunition
These third-party visuals are not extracted Call of Juarez artwork.
